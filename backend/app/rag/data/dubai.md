# Dubai Destination Guide

## Overview
Dubai is a city in the United Arab Emirates known for modern architecture,
desert landscapes, and a mix of traditional souks and luxury shopping malls.
It is a popular short-haul destination for travelers from India, roughly a
3 to 3.5 hour flight from major Indian cities.

## Best Time to Visit
The most comfortable months are November through March, with daytime
temperatures between 20-30C. April to September is very hot (often above
40C), so outdoor activities are best scheduled early morning or evening
during those months.

## Transport
The Dubai Metro is clean, affordable, and covers most tourist areas
including Downtown, Marina, and the Airport. The Nol card is used across
metro, bus, and tram. Taxis and ride-hailing apps are widely available.
Most attractions in Downtown and Marina are walkable from metro stations,
but Old Dubai (Deira, Bur Dubai) is best explored by abra (water taxi)
across the creek.

## Culture & Etiquette
The UAE is a Muslim-majority country. Modest dress is expected in
religious sites and traditional neighborhoods. Public displays of
affection are discouraged. Alcohol is available in licensed venues
(hotels, some restaurants) but not sold in most supermarkets outside
designated zones. During Ramadan, eating and drinking in public during
daylight hours is discouraged.

## Money
The local currency is the UAE Dirham (AED). Most major currencies,
including INR, can be exchanged at malls and exchange counters. Credit
cards are widely accepted; carry some cash for souks and small vendors.

## Neighborhoods & Highlights
- Downtown Dubai: Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall, Dubai Fountain shows.
- Dubai Marina: waterfront promenade, dining, Marina Walk, yacht cruises.
- Old Dubai (Bur Dubai / Deira): Gold Souk, Spice Souk, abra rides,
  Al Fahidi historic district.
- Palm Jumeirah: beach resorts, Atlantis, The View at The Palm.
- Desert areas outside the city: desert safaris, dune bashing, camel
  rides, Bedouin-style camps with dinner and entertainment.

## Airport
Dubai International Airport (DXB) is one of the busiest hubs globally,
roughly 20-30 minutes from Downtown by metro or taxi depending on
traffic. Airport transfers by metro are inexpensive; private taxis take
about 30-45 minutes to Marina during peak traffic.
